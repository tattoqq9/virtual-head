MODEL AND ASSET BUNDLE STATUS

The public distribution does not include ONNX model weights. MODEL_DOWNLOADS.json
contains pinned URLs, sizes, SHA-256 values, project URLs, and license references
for user-initiated downloads from the author's official GitHub Releases. Each user
must review the official project, release, and license pages and decide whether
the intended use is permitted. The copies in licenses/ are supplied only as review
aids; Virtual Head does not determine or guarantee their applicability to model
weights. Alpha.10 uses verified first-run downloads as its release design. A private
local test build may still contain the two models listed in
MODEL_BUNDLE_PLAN.json and is marked DO NOT REDISTRIBUTE. Public and private
bundles do not include third-party Minecraft skin files, VRM/MMD/FBX assets, a
standalone ffmpeg.exe, NVIDIA CUDA/cuDNN/TensorRT libraries, source/test videos,
user settings, or generated outputs. OpenCV's internal FFmpeg video I/O binary
is included and covered by the OpenCV third-party notices above. See
MODEL_LICENSES.md for the model audit and distribution policy.
